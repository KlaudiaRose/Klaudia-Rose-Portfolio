PORTFOLIO - SPACES

Hi, I'm Klaudia Rose, composer and instrumentalist interested in collaborating with film makers, animation artists and game designers.

Here you will find my updated works, mostly film soundtrack-style. I used the below images to inspire the following companion compositions. These compositions correlate to the sound, feeling or story of the image, where the visual and auditory world align. I'm very passionate about exploring and creating space through sound by using experimental mixing techniques. The most important thing for me is to tell a story with sound and bring the project to life.

Graduated Diploma of Sound Production at RMIT - 2026 (Mid Year)

List of services:

- Composed music for films, games, animations and other visual content

- Final Mixes of the track/soundtrack/score

- Recording instruments such as electric guitar, accoustic steel string guitar, violin, cello and piano.

Sound style: Mystical, Creepy, Natural, Warped, Magical, Playful, Otherworldly.
